1. Open a product and click Buy now. (Custom products show Request a quote instead.)
2. Sign in and verify your email if asked.
3. On checkout fill Billing details: country, State, City, Postal code, and GST number (optional).
4. Under Review & discounts enter a Coupon (optional) and check the Total.
5. Choose a Payment method: Pay instantly by card or UPI, or Bank transfer.
6. Click Place order.
For bank transfer, pay using the shown details, then Submit your reference. We confirm within one business day. You will find the order under Purchases & access.
