title: Accept a quote you received
roles: [customer]
keywords: [quote, quotation, accept quote, reject quote, pay link, custom quote, quote accept karo]
---
1. Open the quote link from your email, or go to Quotes in your account.
2. Make sure you are signed in with the same email the quote was sent to. If not, use Switch account.
3. Read the Amount, tax and the Valid until date.
4. Click Accept & pay and complete payment.
5. Not interested? Click Reject quote.
Have a question first? Click Ask a question or Open a query. After you pay, the quote shows Open order and Open purchase. A quote cannot be paid after it expires or is cancelled.
