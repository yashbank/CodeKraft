1. Open Content, then Landing.
2. Edit sections such as Who we are, What we build and What we sell. Change the Hero image with Upload image or Replace image.
3. Set Primary CTA label, Primary CTA target and the secondary CTA.
4. Under Featured products click Add product to pick published products.
5. Save your work. A "Saved n s ago" or "Unsaved changes" note shows the state.
Other pages live under Content too: Services, Case studies, Testimonials & logos, Blog, FAQs and Legal. Staff can edit but only admins can publish.
