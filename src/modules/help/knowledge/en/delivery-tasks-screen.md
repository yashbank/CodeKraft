Delivery tasks lists manual work after a sale: Provision and Revoke external tasks, with Product, Customer, Assigned to and Created.
1. Open Delivery tasks. Use Filter by kind to see Provision or Revoke external.
2. Open the row menu for Open order or Mark done.
3. When done, click Mark done and add a Note if needed.
Tip: the Revocation tasks dashboard card links here.
