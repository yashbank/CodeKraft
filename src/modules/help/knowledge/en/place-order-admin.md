Use this when a customer pays offline or you sell a project directly.
1. Open Orders and click New manual order.
2. Choose Product order (offline sale) or Project order (client invoice).
3. Pick the Registered customer (search by email) or click Invite customer.
4. Click Add line for each offering. Set Currency, Discount amount and Apply tax if needed.
5. Optional: turn on Record payment now and fill Method, Amount received, Reference and Paid on.
6. Click Create order, or Create & confirm when a payment is recorded.
A project order shows Create & request split approval and waits in Approvals first.
