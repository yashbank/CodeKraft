1. Open Settings from the account menu.
2. Under Preferences choose a Theme (light or dark). A "Theme saved" message confirms it.
3. Choose your Display currency. Prices are shown in it.
4. Under Notifications switch on or off Orders and delivery, Queries and support, and Product updates.
You can also change the theme and Display currency from the site header.
