title: Record an expense
roles: [admin, super_admin]
keywords: [expense, cost, spend, receipt, shared expense, kharcha, expense add karo]
---
1. Open Finance, then Expenses.
2. Click Record expense.
3. Fill Description, Category, Amount, Incurred on, and attach a Receipt if you have one.
4. Choose who bears it: Company (no product), a Product, or Shared by split (Share by product split).
5. Save. An "Expense recorded" message appears.
Expenses cannot be edited. To fix one, use Reverse via adjustment. Each row has View receipt and Copy seq for the ledger sequence number.
