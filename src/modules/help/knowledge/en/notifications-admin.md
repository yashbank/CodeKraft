1. Open Notifications (under Overview), or use the bell in the top bar. It shows the unread count.
2. Each item has a quick action such as Confirm payment or Review approval.
3. Click Load more to see older ones.
4. Mark all as read clears the unread count. An "All notifications marked read" message confirms it.
You get notices for new orders, payments to confirm, approvals waiting and new queries or leads. Open the Audit log under System if you need the full history of who did what.
