The Service checklists due widget lists service deliveries with steps still not done, oldest first (up to 10).
1. Open Dashboard and find Service checklists due.
2. Each row shows the product name, a progress bar and text like 2 of 5 steps done.
3. Click a row to open Entitlements and tick off the Service steps.
4. If it says No service checklists due, every service delivery is complete.
Tip: only deliveries with status Pending or Active are counted.
