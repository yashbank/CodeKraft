Refunds and adjustments always need a second person to approve.
Refund:
1. Open Orders and click the order.
2. Click More order actions, then Propose refund.
3. Enter the amount and a reason, then click Request approval.
Adjustment (fixes a ledger mistake):
1. Open Finance, then Adjustments.
2. Click New adjustment, fill Lines and Reason, check Impact preview.
3. Submit it.
Watch the status in Approvals. You cannot approve your own request.
