1. Sign in and open your account.
2. Go to Purchases & access. Use the filters or Search by product name.
3. Open orders waiting for payment show Pay now or Submit reference.
4. Click an order to see Order progress, Order summary and Paid on.
5. Statuses include Pending payment, Confirmed by us, Access ready and Partially refunded.
When access is ready click Go to your purchase. Past payments are also under Invoices & payments, with Open order beside each line. Still stuck? Click Open a query.
