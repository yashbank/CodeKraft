Testimonials & logos manages customer quotes and client logos on the site.
1. Open Content, then Testimonials & logos.
2. In Testimonials, add Quote, Author name, Author title, Company and Product.
3. In Client logos, add Name, a logo image and a Link URL.
4. Use Move earlier and Move later to order, and Published to show.
