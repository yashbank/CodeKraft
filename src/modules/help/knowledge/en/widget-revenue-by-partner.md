The Revenue by partner widget is a bar chart of partner allocations per partner (top 8) for the selected range. It is for Super Admin only.
1. Open Dashboard and pick a range.
2. Read the bars to compare partners.
3. Click View all to open Reports & statements, or open Allocations for the split of each order.
