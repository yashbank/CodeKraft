A quote becomes an order when the customer accepts and pays.
1. Open Quotes and click New quote.
2. Choose the Customer, set the Title, Currency, Price (tax-exclusive) and Tax %. Optionally pick a Linked offering.
3. Set Valid until (default is 14 days).
4. Click Send to customer, or Save draft to finish later.
5. After sending, copy the Pay link, or use Resend to customer.
6. The customer opens the quote and clicks Accept & pay. The quote then shows its Order number.
You can also start one from a lead with Send custom quote.
