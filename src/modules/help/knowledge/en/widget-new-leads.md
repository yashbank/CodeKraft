The New leads widget shows how many leads still have status New, plus the newest ones.
1. Open Dashboard and find New leads.
2. The big number is the total of leads with status New that you can see. A line under it compares with before.
3. Below it, up to 5 leads show with company or email. Click one to open it.
4. Click View all to open Leads, then use Assign to me to claim one.
Tip: admins and staff see leads assigned to them plus the unassigned pool.
