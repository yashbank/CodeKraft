The Split approvals pending widget lists project-order splits waiting for your approval. Your own requests never show here.
1. Open Dashboard and find Split approvals pending.
2. Each row shows the request summary and how many hours ago it was requested.
3. Click a row or View all to open Approvals and decide.
Tip: an empty card says No split approvals pending.
