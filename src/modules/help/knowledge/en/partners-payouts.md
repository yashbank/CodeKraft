1. Open Finance, then Partners & payouts.
2. To add a partner click Add partner, pick an Admin user (or enter Admin email) and set Display name.
3. Read Partner balances: Earned (all time), Paid out and Closing.
4. To pay one, click Record payout. Choose the partner, enter Amount, Date paid and Bank reference, then submit.
The payout goes to Approvals ("Payout sent for approval") and posts to the ledger once approved. Use Payout status to filter Awaiting approval.
