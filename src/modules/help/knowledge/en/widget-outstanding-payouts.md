The Outstanding payouts widget shows the total owed to partners (allocated minus refunded minus paid out, all time) and the top 6 partners.
1. Open Dashboard and find Outstanding payouts.
2. The big number is the total across all partners. Each row shows a partner and the amount owed.
3. Click a row or View all to open Partners & payouts and record a payout.
Tip: an empty card says Nothing outstanding.
