The Overdue follow-ups widget lists leads whose follow-up date has passed and that are not yet Won or Lost.
1. Open Dashboard. Find the card titled Overdue follow-ups.
2. Each row shows the lead name with the company or email under it, marked with an amber dot. Up to 5 rows show.
3. Click a row to open that lead. Click View all at the bottom to open Leads.
4. On the lead, use Set follow-up to pick a new date, or Add note and change the status.
5. If the card says No overdue follow-ups, nothing is late.
Tip: staff and admin see only leads assigned to them or in the unassigned pool.
