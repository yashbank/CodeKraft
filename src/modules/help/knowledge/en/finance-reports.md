Reports & statements builds finance reports and partner statements.
1. Open Finance, then Reports & statements.
2. Pick a period with the presets This month, Last month, This FY, Last FY or Custom.
3. Choose Partner, Currency and Format.
4. Click Generate statement. Generated statements lists past ones; use Regenerate (5-min link) to download again.
Tip: partners see only their own statements.
