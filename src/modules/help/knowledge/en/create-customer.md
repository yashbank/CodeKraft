Create an account for someone who has not signed up yet.
1. Open Customers.
2. Click New customer.
3. Fill Name and Email. Phone and Company are optional.
4. Submit the dialog. The customer gets an email to set a password.
5. If the email fails, a warning appears. Open the customer and use Reset link / password to send it again.
After this you can pick the customer in New manual order or New quote. Staff can view customers but cannot create them.
