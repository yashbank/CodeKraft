Allocations shows how each order was split: Gross, Discount, Tax, Bank charge, Distributable, Company cut and each partner share.
1. Open Finance, then Allocations.
2. Filter by Product, Partner, Ownership version or From date.
3. Click Expand on a row to see the breakdown.
Tip: partners see only their own share.
