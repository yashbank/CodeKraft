After payment, deliver what the customer bought.
1. Open Delivery tasks to see open work. Use Assign to, then Mark done when finished.
2. For access or keys, open Entitlements.
3. Expand a row to use Enter licence key, Replace licence key, Reset downloads or Revoke external.
4. For services, work through Service steps.
5. When everything is handed over, click Mark delivered and add a Note to customer.
You can also reach the same actions from the order page under Items & fulfilment. The Fulfilment guide in Settings explains each delivery type.
