The Publish approvals pending widget lists product publish requests waiting for your decision. Your own requests never show here.
1. Open Dashboard and find Publish approvals pending.
2. Each row shows what is to be published and how many hours ago it was requested.
3. Click a row or View all to open Approvals and approve or reject it.
Tip: an empty card says No publish approvals pending.
