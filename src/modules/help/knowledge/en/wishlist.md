1. Sign in. Without an account the heart icon says Sign in to add to wishlist.
2. On any product card click the heart (Add to wishlist). Click it again to take it off (Remove from wishlist).
3. Open Wishlist from your account menu.
4. Use Sort wishlist for Recently added, Name or Price.
5. For each item click Buy now (or Request a quote), View product, or Remove.
Prices follow your Display currency. Your list is saved to your account, so it is there on every device.
