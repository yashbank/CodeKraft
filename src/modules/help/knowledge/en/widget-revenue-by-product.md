The Revenue by product widget is a bar chart of net revenue per product for the selected range.
1. Open Dashboard and pick a range.
2. Read the bars to see which product earns most.
3. Click a bar to open the Ledger filtered to that product and range.
Tip: View all opens Reports & statements.
