1. Open Finance (left menu). It expands into Ledger, Allocations, Partners & payouts, Expenses, Adjustments and Reports & statements.
2. Ledger lists every Entry. Filter by Entry type, Party type or Order number. Entries are Immutable, so errors are fixed with an adjustment.
3. Allocations shows how each sale splits: Company cut, Gateway fee, Bank charge and partner shares.
4. In Reports & statements pick a period (This month, Last month, This FY), choose Partner, then click Generate statement and Download.
Partners see only their own lines.
