Customers lists every customer account with Customer, Company / country, Status, Purchases, Lifetime spend, Open items, Joined and Last seen.
1. Open Customers. Use the filters Status, Country, Tag, Has open order and Has active subscription.
2. Open the row menu for Open, New quote, New manual order or Reset link / password.
3. Open a customer to see their orders, queries and notes.
Tip: status can be Active, Suspended or Deleted.
