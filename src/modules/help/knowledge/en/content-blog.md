Blog manages posts on the public blog.
1. Open Content, then Blog. Click Edit on a post or add one.
2. Fill Title, Slug, Excerpt, Body, Cover image and optionally Product.
3. Add SEO title and SEO description.
4. Save, and set Published when ready.
