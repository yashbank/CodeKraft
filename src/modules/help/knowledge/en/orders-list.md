Orders has an Order queue of orders awaiting confirmation and a Full orders list.
1. Open Orders. The Order queue shows orders with Awaiting confirmation status.
2. Open the row menu for Open, Confirm payment, Mark failed, Cancel or Download invoice.
3. Use filters such as Status, Type, Method and From to narrow the Full orders list.
4. Click an order to see items, payment, invoice and timeline.
Tip: the Payments awaiting confirmation dashboard card links here.
