Invoices are issued automatically when a payment is confirmed. There is no separate button to draft one.
1. Open Orders and click the order.
2. Confirm the order is paid (use Confirm payment first if it is not).
3. Click More order actions.
4. Choose Download invoice.
The option stays disabled until the order has an invoice number. Refunds produce a credit note automatically. The customer sees the same PDF under Invoices & payments.
