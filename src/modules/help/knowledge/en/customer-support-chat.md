Chat:
1. Open Chat from your account menu and ask about orders or products.
2. For a person, click Talk to a human. A query is created and the team answers there.
3. Use New conversation for a fresh start. Past conversations are kept.
Queries:
1. Open Queries and click New query.
2. Write your message, attach files if needed, and send.
3. Reply under Write a reply. Waiting on you means we need your answer.
4. When fixed, click Mark resolved.
You must verify your email before chatting.
