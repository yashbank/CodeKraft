The Payments awaiting confirmation widget lists orders where the customer submitted a payment reference and the order is still Pending payment, oldest first.
1. Open Dashboard and find Payments awaiting confirmation.
2. Each row shows the order number, customer name, amount and the text reference submitted.
3. Click a row to open the order, check the reference against your bank, then confirm the payment.
4. View all opens Orders.
Tip: only people who can confirm payments see this card.
