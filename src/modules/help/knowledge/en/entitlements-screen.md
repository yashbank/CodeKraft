Entitlements lists what customers have access to, with Customer, Product, Type, Status, Access ends, Downloads and Provisioning.
1. Open Entitlements. Filter by kind and status.
2. Open the row menu for Extend, Revoke, Reset downloads, Service steps, Mark delivered, Create delivery task or Open order.
3. For services, open Service steps and mark each step done.
Tip: Cap reached means the download limit is used.
