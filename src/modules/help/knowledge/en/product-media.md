title: Add images, video or files to a product
roles: [staff, admin, super_admin]
keywords: [media, image, photo, video, upload, cover image, gallery, photo lagao, image upload]
---
1. Open Products and click the product.
2. Go to the Media tab.
3. Click Choose file. Images, MP4/WebM/MOV video and PDF are accepted.
4. Add Alt text (optional) for accessibility.
5. Click Upload & attach. A Media attached message appears.
6. To use an image as the thumbnail, click Use cover image.
The Cover image appears on product cards. Remove an item from the same tab if it is wrong. Release downloads go in the Versions tab with Upload release file.
