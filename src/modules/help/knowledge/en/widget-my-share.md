The My share widget shows your own allocations in the selected range, net of refunds, with a weekly trend line.
1. Open Dashboard and pick a range.
2. Read the amount on My share. The hint says Your allocations, net of refunds.
3. For the details per order, open Ledger or Allocations.
Tip: the amount covers only your own share, not other partners.
