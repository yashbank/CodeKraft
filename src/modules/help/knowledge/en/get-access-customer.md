1. Open Purchases & access (also called Your products).
2. Find the product card. Its status chip shows if access is active.
3. Use the button on the card: Reveal key, Open instructions, View progress or Manage subscription, depending on the type.
4. For more, click the card for details: License type, Version owned and Access period.
5. If access ended, click Renew or Buy again.
Something wrong or not working? Click Contact support. Downloads can have a limit, and the details page shows what is left.
