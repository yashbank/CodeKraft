Change details:
1. Open Settings from the account menu.
2. Under Profile edit your name and Phone, or use Change email (a verification link is sent).
3. Under Billing details save your address and GST number.
Change password:
1. In Change password enter your current and new password, confirm it, then click Update password.
Forgot it? On the sign-in page click Forgot password? and follow the Reset your password email.
Click Sign out other sessions if you used a shared device. Delete account closes your account for good.
