1. Click the bell in the top bar. The number shows unread items.
2. Click Mark all read to clear them, or View all to open the full page.
3. On Notifications use Filter by type (for example Product updates) and the Unread filter.
4. Click an item to jump to the order, query or product it is about.
You are told when a payment is confirmed, when access is ready, when support replies and when a product you own is updated. To stop some of these emails, go to Settings, then Notifications.
