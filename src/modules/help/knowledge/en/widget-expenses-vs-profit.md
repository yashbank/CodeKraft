The Expenses vs profit widget is a stacked bar chart of expenses and profit for each of the last 6 months.
1. Open Dashboard and find Expenses vs profit.
2. Read each month: expenses in one colour, profit in the other. Profit below zero is shown as zero.
3. Click View all to open Expenses and add or check expenses.
Tip: expenses in other currencies are converted to INR.
