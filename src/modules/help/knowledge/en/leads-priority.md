1. Open Leads. Use the Unassigned pool filter to find new ones.
2. Use the row actions: Assign to me to claim a lead.
3. Choose Set priority and pick Normal or High.
4. Choose Set follow-up and a date. Overdue only shows missed follow-ups.
5. Open a lead to Add note, check the Chat transcript or Original message.
6. Move it forward with Change status, Send custom quote or Create project order.
7. If it will not close, choose Mark lost and give a reason.
Add a lead yourself with New lead (source is Manual).
