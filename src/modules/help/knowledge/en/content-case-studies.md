Case studies lists client stories with Title, Client, Industry, Status and Updated.
1. Open Content, then Case studies.
2. Click Edit or add one. Fill Client name, Industry, Problem, Result highlight, Cover image and Gallery.
3. Set status Draft or Published and Save.
4. Use Move up and Move down to order them.
