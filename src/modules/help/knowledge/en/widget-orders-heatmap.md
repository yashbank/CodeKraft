The Orders by weekday and hour widget is a heatmap of when orders arrived in the last 90 days, in Indian Standard Time.
1. Open Dashboard and find Orders by weekday and hour.
2. Darker cells mean more orders at that weekday and hour.
3. Use it to plan when to be available for support.
Tip: Super Admin only. It ignores the range selector.
