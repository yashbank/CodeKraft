The Revocation tasks widget lists open Revoke external delivery tasks: external accounts to disable after a refund or revocation.
1. Open Dashboard and find Revocation tasks.
2. Each row shows the product name and the text Disable the external account.
3. Click a row or View all to open Delivery tasks.
4. Disable the account outside the system, then click Mark done on the task.
Tip: an empty card says No revocation tasks.
