Warning: this permanently deletes data and cannot be undone.
1. Open Settings, then Danger zone.
2. Under Data to delete tick what to remove. Use Preview row counts to see Rows to delete.
3. Click Download backup. The request button stays disabled until you do.
4. Click Request site reset.
5. In the confirmation dialog type the site name exactly (the "Type ... to confirm" box), then click Confirm site reset request.
Nothing is deleted yet: a different super admin must approve it in Approvals. Super admin accounts, settings, roles, legal pages and the audit log are always kept.
