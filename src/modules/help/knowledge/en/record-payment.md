Use this for bank transfer or UPI orders waiting for confirmation.
1. Open Orders and find the order (the queue shows Awaiting confirmation).
2. Click Confirm payment.
3. Fill Received on and Reference (UTR / NEFT). Add a Note if useful.
4. Check the Payment recap, then click Confirm and mark Paid.
The order becomes paid, the invoice is created and the customer gets access. If money never arrived, use Mark failed and give a Reason instead.
