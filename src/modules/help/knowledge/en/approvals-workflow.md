Sensitive changes need a second person. These include product publish, project order split, ledger adjustment, payouts, ownership change, admin user change and Reset site data.
1. Open Approvals (under Overview). The badge shows how many wait for you.
2. Filter by type, then click a request.
3. Read What will change, Before and After, and Who must approve.
4. Click Approve or Reject.
Requests you made appear in your own list, where you can use Cancel request. You can never approve your own request.
