Only a super admin can manage admin users.
1. Open Admin users (under System).
2. Invite: click Invite admin, enter Email, choose Role (Super Admin, Admin or Staff). Turn on Also a partner if needed. The invite goes to Approvals ("Invite approval requested").
3. Change role: open the row actions and choose Change role, then pick the New role.
4. Remove: choose Delete account… and confirm.
Role changes and removals also need approval. The last super admin cannot be deleted.
