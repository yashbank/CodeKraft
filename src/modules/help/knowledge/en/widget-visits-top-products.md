The Visits & top products widget shows total page and product views for the range and the 5 most viewed products.
1. Open Dashboard and pick a range.
2. The big number is views. The table lists Product and Visits.
3. It shows No product views yet when nothing was viewed.
Tip: this card is for Super Admin only.
