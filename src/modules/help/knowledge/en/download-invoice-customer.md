1. Sign in and open Invoices & payments from the account menu.
2. Use All years to narrow the list if needed.
3. Find the row. The Invoice no, Date, Order and Status are shown.
4. Click PDF. The invoice opens in a new tab, and you can save or print it.
Invoices appear once your payment is confirmed. Bank transfers can take up to one business day. If the PDF fails, wait a minute and try again, or Open a query from Queries. Refunds show as credit notes in the same list.
