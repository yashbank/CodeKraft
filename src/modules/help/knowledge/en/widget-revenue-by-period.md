The Revenue by period widget is a line chart of net revenue per week or month for the selected range, in INR.
1. Open Dashboard and choose a range at the top: Last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or This FY.
2. Click View all to open Reports & statements for detail.
Tip: it says no data when there was no revenue in the range.
