You can choose which widgets the Dashboard shows, in what order, and for what date range.
1. Open Dashboard. Use the Range list for Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days or This FY.
2. Click Customise to open the widget library and switch widgets on or off. You only see widgets your role may use.
3. Click Edit layout to drag cards or use the move earlier and move later buttons. Click Done when finished.
4. Use the refresh icon on a card to reload only that card, or its menu to Remove it.
5. Click Reset to default to restore the standard layout.
Tip: your layout is saved for your own account.
