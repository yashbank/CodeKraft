The Open queries widget lists customer support queries with status Open, up to 10, so you can reply fast.
1. Open Dashboard and find Open queries.
2. Each row shows the query subject and the customer name or email.
3. Click a row to open the thread and reply. Click View all to open Queries.
4. If it says Inbox zero, no query is open.
Tip: staff and admins see only queries assigned to them or in the pool.
