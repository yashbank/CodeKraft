Only a super admin can change this.
1. Open Settings and go to General / seller.
2. Find Site phase.
3. Choose one:
- Live: the normal public site.
- Testing: shows a Test site banner, asks search engines not to index, and does not email customers.
- Maintenance: visitors see a back soon page; admin and payments keep working.
4. Click Save changes.
Use Testing while trying things out, Maintenance for downtime, and switch back to Live before launch.
