title: Manage product categories
roles: [staff, admin, super_admin]
keywords: [category, categories, sub category, tag, group products, category banao]
---
1. Open Categories (under Catalog), or use the Category tree beside the Products list.
2. Click Category to add one. Choose a Parent to make it a sub-category (maximum depth is 2).
3. Click an existing category to rename or edit it, then save.
4. To remove one, select it and click Delete.
Delete is blocked while any product still uses the category. Move those products to another category first. Use All categories on the Products list to filter by category.
