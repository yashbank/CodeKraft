The Conversion rate widget shows Won divided by closed leads (Won plus Lost) for the selected range.
1. Open Dashboard and pick a range (Last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or This FY).
2. Read the percentage on the Conversion rate card. The line under it says how many were won of how many closed.
3. It shows No data yet when no lead was closed in that range.
Tip: the range chip on the card tells which range is used.
