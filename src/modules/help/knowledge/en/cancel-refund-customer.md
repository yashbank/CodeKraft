title: Cancel an order or ask for a refund
roles: [customer]
keywords: [cancel order, refund, money back, return, refund request, cancel karo, refund chahiye, paisa wapas]
---
1. Open Purchases & access and click the order.
2. Not paid yet? Click Cancel order.
3. Already paid? Click Request refund, write Tell us why (optional), then Send request.
4. A "Refund request sent" message appears. If one is already open you will see "Refund request already open".
We review it, and an approver must sign off before money moves. Track the thread in Queries. A confirmed refund shows as Partially refunded or refunded, with a credit note in Invoices & payments.
