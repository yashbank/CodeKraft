roles: [admin, super_admin]

---
1. Open Chatbot (under Growth).
2. Check Usage for Daily chatbot messages, Estimated cost and Users at cap.
3. Open Conversations to read a Chat transcript. Use Open lead or Open escalated query to follow up.
4. Under Prompts edit the System prompt, add Version notes and save a new Prompt version.
5. Use Try a message to test the bot before relying on it.
Platform cap and Escalation rate show whether the bot is hitting limits or handing too many chats to humans.
